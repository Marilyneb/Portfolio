Shrimp
Republic.

Brand applications, menu design, packaging and social campaigns.

MenusPackagingCampaignsMotionConcept
Project scope

My work for Shrimp Republic spans printed menus, takeaway packaging, sauce labels and social content. The challenge was to carry a playful seafood brand across practical restaurant touchpoints while keeping product information clear and recognizable.

Deliverables

Print · Packaging · Social design · Video

01 / Menu design

Menu design & evolution

The original menu established a visual hierarchy for categories, dishes and prices. The updated edition accommodates an expanded food offering while retaining the brand’s bold typography and orange-and-blue palette.

02 / Brand applications

Packaging & label design

Packaging layouts and a coordinated sauce-label system bring the restaurant’s visual language into the dining and takeaway experience. The individual labels use distinct colors and illustrations to differentiate flavors.
